========= Text based Dungeon Crawler =========

            Basic idea and structure.
            Made as a vauge blueprint before coding.


A console game where the player explores [6] rooms,
 fights [3] kind of enemies,
 collects gear and gold, 
 and wins by [defeating a boss] or loses by [dying].


 Things needed:
 - player
 - rooms
 - enemies
 - gold
 - items
 


 Classes:
 - game
 - player
 - enemy
    - boss
 - item
    - gear
    - potion


 Class components:
 - enum: gear_slot (head, chest, gloves, pants, footwear, hand)
 - enum: direction (north, east, south, west)
 
- game
   - player: player
   - list: rooms
   - bool: is_running

 - player
    - int: health
    - int: max_health
    - int: attack
    - int: current_gold
    - list: inventory
    - room: current_room
    - dictionary: equipped_gear (slot -> gear, empty if nothing equipped)

 - room
    - string: name
    - list: enemies
    - list: items
    - dictionary: exits (direction -> room)

 - enemy
    - string: name
    - int: health
    - int: attack
    - int: gold_reward
    - item: loot (can be empty)
    - float: armor

- item
    - string: name
    - int: value

- gear
    - enum: gear_slot
    - float: armor
    - int: attack

- potion
    - int: heal_amount



------ Commands ------
The player uses written commands to make decicions, interact and act.
commands are not case sensitive, take bfg = Take BFG

Commands:
   - go [north, east, west, south]
   - look (descirbes the room)
   - look [at enemy]
   - take [sword]
   - equip [sword]
   - attack [enemy]
   - use [potion]
   - buy [potion]
   - sell [potion]
   - inventory
   - quit
   - unknown input



------ Combat & Armor ------
Combat is turn based, the player and enemies engaged in a fight each take turns to attack or act (use items etc.).
After a combat encounter enemies may drop loot such as gold or items.

Damage numbers are rounded.
An attack could possibly inflict 0 dmg.

Armor reduces incoming damage (dmg) by a percentage.

Dmg reduction % = Armor / (Armor + K)

K is a constant with the value 100.

Example: 
   - Armor = 16.0
   - Dmg reduction % = 16.0 / (16.0 + 100.0) = 16.0 / 116.0 = 0.138 = 13.8%

   The armor reduces the incoming dmg by 13.8%



------ Boss ------
The Boss is an enemy with special behavior and when killed, the player wins the game.

The Boss could have 2 phases, the second phase begins when health < 50%, the boss then heals and gets a dmg buff
A phase can not repeat.



------ Rules ------
 - Player total attack = base attack + attack of all equipped gear
 - Player total armor  = sum of armor of all equipped gear (calculated, not stored)
 - Killing an enemy: player gets its gold_reward, loot drops into the room, enemy is removed from the room
 - Shop: items there are bought with `buy`, not `take`. `buy` costs the item's value. `sell` pays half the value. Buy and sell only work in the Shop.
 - Using a potion takes your turn in combat. It can't heal past max_health.
 - Killing the boss ends the game with a win. Health reaching 0 for the player ends it with a loss.
